Divaran – Online Marketplace Platform

Divaran is a marketplace platform inspired by classified advertisement websites, designed to provide a seamless experience for buying, selling, and discovering products. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

I was responsible for the end-to-end UI/UX design of the product, from research and wireframing to the final high-fidelity interfaces.

The platform enables users to create and manage advertisements, browse listings, search products based on categories, cities, and keywords, and connect with sellers through an intuitive user experience.
