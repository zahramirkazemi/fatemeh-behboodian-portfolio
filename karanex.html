Karanex – Cryptocurrency Trading & Learning Platform

Karanex is a cryptocurrency platform that combines digital asset trading with educational content, allowing users to trade cryptocurrencies, purchase investment courses, and manage their financial activities in one place.

As a UI/UX Designer, I contributed to several key sections of the platform, focusing on creating intuitive, data-rich, and scalable user experiences.

01 — DATA-RICH, EDGE TO EDGE

Dense financial screens, kept legible.

Charts, order books and tables carry real numbers on every screen. Mobile keeps the same hierarchy, one column at a time.

Spot trading, with the order book at hand

DESKTOP + MOBILE

Live candlestick chart, order book and a buy/sell panel with a portfolio summary, all visible without switching screens. Mobile swaps to tabs while keeping chart, book and order form reachable.
