THE STORY — 03 CHAPTERS
Composition is composition, whether it is paint or a payment flow.
CHAPTER 01 — WHERE I STARTED

I spent four years painting Persian miniatures.

A BA in Islamic art teaches you to hold a whole composition in your head and to fix one millimetre at a time. It turns out both habits are the job.

yes, this is why I love spacing tokens
CHAPTER 02 — THE SWITCH

Then I wanted the picture to work for someone else.

I started with landing pages, moved to a municipal website, then a platform for children finding their talents. Each one taught me that clarity beats decoration.

CHAPTER 03 — NOW

Multi-role platforms, before anyone has seen them.

At Vista App I design exchanges, marketplaces and booking flows that have three kinds of user and no launch date yet. Design systems are how I keep them coherent.
